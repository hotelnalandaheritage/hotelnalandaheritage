Hotel near Rajgir Glass Bridge

The Rajgir Glass Bridge, inside the Nature Safari area, is one of Bihar's newest attractions. Our hotel is a short drive away (about 6–7 km by road, confirm with our front desk), so you can go early and avoid queues.

Tips

  • Visit in the morning for lighter crowds.
  • The Zoo Safari is a separate ticket and place, so plan both.
  • Ask our front desk to book a taxi or auto.
