Frequently asked questions

What are the check-in and check-out times?

Check-in is from 12:00 PM and check-out is 11:00 AM. Early check-in or late check-out can be arranged on request, subject to availability.

Is free parking available?

Yes. Free on-site parking is available for cars and two-wheelers.

Is the hotel close to Rajgir Railway Station?

Rajgir Railway Station is about an 18-minute walk. Autos and taxis are easily available, and our front desk can arrange pickup.

Can you arrange sightseeing in Rajgir and Nalanda?

Yes. We arrange taxis and local guides for the Glass Bridge, Ropeway, Vishwa Shanti Stupa, Hot Springs, Nalanda ruins and Bodh Gaya.

What is the cancellation policy?

Free cancellation up to 24 hours before check-in. Later cancellations or no-shows are charged one night.

What ID is needed at check-in?

A valid government photo ID such as Aadhaar, passport, voter ID or driving licence.

Are pets allowed?

Small, well-behaved pets are allowed on request. Please tell us before arrival.
