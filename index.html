How to Reach Us

By Air

Fly into Jay Prakash Narayan Airport, Patna (~95 km) or Gaya Airport (~85 km). Prepaid taxis and cabs run regularly to Rajgir from both — around 2 to 2.5 hours by road.

By Rail

Rajgir Railway Station is the closest, just 5 minutes from the hotel, with connections via Patna and Bakhtiyarpur. Gaya Junction is also a well-connected alternative.

By Road

Rajgir sits on a well-maintained state highway, roughly 100 km from Patna and 80 km from Gaya via Bodh Gaya. State buses and private taxis serve the route daily.

Best time to visit Rajgir is October to March, when the weather is cool and comfortable for sightseeing across the hills. Let our front desk know your arrival details and we're happy to arrange a pickup.
