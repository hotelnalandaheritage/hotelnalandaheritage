About the Venue

Your celebration, in the heart of Rajgir

Destination Banquet Cum Marriage Hall brings together a bright indoor hall and a richly draped pandal setting, so your event can be as intimate or as grand as you plan it.

From the floral mandap to the decorated stage, seating and buffet arrangement, everything is set up to make your guests feel welcome — and guests travelling in for the occasion can stay at The Nalanda Heritage hotel.
