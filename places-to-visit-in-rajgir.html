Places to visit in Rajgir

Rajgir, the ancient Rajagriha, is Bihar's home of Buddhist and Jain heritage, hot springs and new adventure parks. Two days is enough for the main spots. Best season: October to March. Distances are approximate from The Nalanda Heritage.

Nature & adventure

Rajgir Glass Bridge (Nature Safari)

India's thrilling glass skywalk inside the Rajgir Nature Safari, with a suspension bridge, zip line and wall climbing. Not allowed for children under 6 or under 4 ft.

From our hotel: about 6–7 km by road. Tip: Book only on the official site, rajgirsafari.bihar.gov.in. Tickets cannot be cancelled.

Rajgir Zoo Safari

Bihar's first zoo safari. Ride a safari vehicle through tiger, leopard, lion, bear and herbivore zones. Plan 2 to 3 hours including the wait.

From our hotel: about 5 km. Tip: The Zoo Safari and Glass Bridge are separate places, about 6 km apart, with separate tickets.

Rajgir Ropeway

One of India's oldest ropeways. A single-seater chairlift lifts you over the forest to Ratnagiri Hill, and a cabin ropeway also runs.

From our hotel: about 4–5 km. Tip: Both ropeways break for lunch. The cabin ropeway is closed on the 2nd and 4th Tuesday.

Vishwa Shanti Stupa (Japanese Peace Pagoda)

A white marble peace pagoda on Ratnagiri Hill, about 400 m high, with four golden Buddha statues and wide views over Rajgir.

From our hotel: about 4–5 km. Tip: Go in the morning for cool weather and clear views.

Ghora Katora Lake

A horseshoe-shaped lake surrounded by forested hills, with a large Buddha statue. Quiet, good for photos and boating.

From our hotel: about 10 km. Tip: Combine with a drive through the Pant Wildlife Sanctuary area.

Heritage & pilgrimage

Brahmakund Hot Springs

Sacred natural hot springs at the foot of Vaibhar Hill, fed by the seven Saptadhara streams. Brahmakund is the hottest, around 45°C.

From our hotel: about 3 km. Tip: Entry rules apply at the Brahmakund enclosure. Ask our front desk. Avoid priests who approach tourists.

Venu Vana (Bamboo Grove)

A peaceful bamboo garden. The Buddha is believed to have stayed here after King Bimbisara gifted him the royal park.

From our hotel: within 3 km. Tip: Small entry fee. A good short walk after the hot springs.

Griddhakuta Peak (Vulture Peak)

A sacred hill where the Buddha is said to have given many sermons. Buddhist pilgrims from across Asia come here.

From our hotel: about 5 km. Tip: Wear sturdy shoes. The climb is gentle but uneven.

Saptaparni Cave

A cave on Vaibhar Hill, about a 1,000-step climb from the hot springs. The first Buddhist Council is said to have met here.

From our hotel: about 3–4 km. Tip: Climb early and carry water.

Son Bhandar Caves

Two rock-cut caves with ancient inscriptions. Local legend says a hidden treasure lies behind the smooth rock wall.

From our hotel: about 3–4 km. Tip: Combine with the Cyclopean Wall nearby.

Cyclopean Wall

An ancient stone wall that once ran about 40 km around old Rajgir (Rajagriha). One of India's oldest known fortification walls.

From our hotel: about 3 km. Tip: Best seen by taxi along the road to Son Bhandar.

Ajatashatru Fort and Stupa

Ruins linked to Ajatashatru, king of Magadha, near the hotel. A short stop for history lovers.

From our hotel: about 1 km. Tip: Quick visit, 30 minutes is enough.

Bimbisara Jail

The ruins where King Bimbisara was held by his son Ajatashatru, according to Buddhist tradition. A small, quiet historic site.

From our hotel: about 3 km. Tip: Pair with Griddhakuta Peak, which is nearby.

Makhdum Kund

A dargah and hot water spring dedicated to the Sufi saint Makhdum Shah, near the Brahmakund area.

From our hotel: about 3 km. Tip: Dress modestly and cover your head.

Jain Temples of Rajgir

Around 26 Jain temples sit on the hills around Rajgir, linked to Lord Mahavira. A key stop for the Jain pilgrimage circuit.

From our hotel: hill temples around town. Tip: Ask us to arrange a guide for the hill temple circuit.

Day trips & local flavour

Nalanda University Ruins

UNESCO World Heritage ruins of the great ancient Buddhist university, with monasteries, stupas and the nearby museum.

From our hotel: about 18 km. Tip: The museum is usually closed on Fridays. Hire a guide for the history.

Bodh Gaya (Mahabodhi Temple)

Where the Buddha attained enlightenment under the Bodhi Tree. A UNESCO World Heritage Site and a must on the Buddhist circuit.

From our hotel: about 75 km. Tip: Allow a full day. We can arrange a taxi.

Silao Khaja Sweet

The famous crisp, layered khaja sweet of Silao, on the road between Rajgir and Nalanda. A classic gift to carry home.

From our hotel: on the road to Nalanda. Tip: Buy fresh from shops that fry it on the spot.

Rajgir Mahotsav (Dance Festival)

The annual Rajgir Dance Festival, held in December, brings classical music and dance to Rajgir.

From our hotel: festival in the town. Tip: Rooms fill fast in festival week. Book early.
